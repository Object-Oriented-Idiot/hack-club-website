long desc:
Meet every [day of week] after shcool in [classroom]. Code at your own pace. present your project. Get real life prizes. Basically, we are a community of ‘hackers’. But this shouldn’t be confused with the illegal form of hacking we all see in movies. Our definition of a ‘hacker’ is someone who solves problems efficiently by developing creative coding solutions. The goal of Hack Club is to help you become a hacker. We want a space at every school where people are making interesting things with code, every week. Most coding classes teach you programming concepts instead of how to write real code. It's like trying to learn carpentry without any wood. So at Hack Club, you learn to code entirely through building things. You start with no experience and build and ship a project every meeting. Clubs also run and attend hackathons(coding marathons), join year-long programs, and grow with others.
