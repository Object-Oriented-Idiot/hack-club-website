Are you interested in Hack Club?

We Meet every [day of week] after school in [classroom].
Code at your own pace, present your project and get real life prizes!
The goal of Hack Club is to help you become a strong coder. We want a space at every school where people are making interesting things with code, every week. Most coding classes teach you programming concepts instead of how to write real code. It's like trying to learn painting without any paint!
Basically, we are part of a wider community of ‘hackers’. But this shouldn’t be confused with the illegal form of hacking we all see in movies. HQ's definition of a ‘hacker’ is someone who solves problems efficiently by developing creative coding solutions.
So at Hack Club, you learn to code entirely through building things. You start with no experience and build and ship a project every meeting.

Disclamer: We can not legally teach Offensive Security to anyone under the age of 16.
